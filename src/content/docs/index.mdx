I work as a user assistance developer at Oracle. I write educational web content for developers who need to connect Oracle products to other systems. I currently own the SOA Suite documentation — the topics in that library — and I maintain the Oracle Integration adapter finder. I transferred to that team from Oracle Health, where I documented known issues, releases, and user workflows. Before that I worked in Oracle Design on research and design teams building a suite of healthcare information management apps. I joined Oracle through its acquisition of Cerner.

I was born in Kansas City, grew up in Kansas, and now live in Brooklyn. I have a Bachelor of Arts in English Language and Literature from the University of Missouri–Kansas City. I've invested a lot of my time in libraries. As an undergraduate, I worked on a research team that investigated rare books held at the Spencer Art Reference Library at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art. Earlier in my career, I occasionally worked nights and weekends as a substitute library assistant at the Kansas City Public Library. I currently volunteer as an English language conversation coordinator at the Queens Public Library at Ridgewood. Queens is the most linguistically diverse place in the world, so working at the library gives me the opportunity to learn more about world languages and cultures.
