I write educational web content for developers who need to connect Oracle products to other systems. I own the SOA Suite documentation library and I maintain the Oracle Integration adapter finder.

I joined Cerner in 2021 and Oracle through its acquisition of Cerner the following year. I worked in Oracle Design on research and design teams building a suite of healthcare information management apps. I then documented known issues, releases, and user workflows for Oracle Health before moving to integrations.

I live in Brooklyn. I have a Bachelor of Arts in English Language and Literature from the University of Missouri–Kansas City. As an undergraduate I worked on a research team that investigated rare books at the Spencer Art Reference Library at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art. I co-authored a [peer-reviewed article](https://differentvisions.org/homage-to-medieval-langres/) on our findings.
