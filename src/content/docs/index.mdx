I was born in Kansas City, grew up in the Kansas suburbs, and now live in Brooklyn. I work as a senior user assistance developer at Oracle, where I focus on application and data integrations. I create educational web content for developers who need to connect Oracle database products to other solutions. I transferred to that team from the Oracle Health product organization, where I worked as a technical writer documenting known issues, releases, and user workflows. I did a stint in Oracle Design, working on research and design teams to develop a highly-accessible and modern suite of healthcare information management apps. I joined Oracle through its acquisition of Cerner.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts in English Language and Literature, with a minor in Medieval and Early Modern Studies, from the University of Missouri—Kansas City. I'm passionate about libraries. As an undergraduate, I worked on a research team that investigated rare books held at the Spencer Art Reference Library at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art. Early in my career at Oracle, I occasionally worked nights and weekends as a substitute library assistant at the Kansas City Public Library. I currently volunteer as an English language conversation coordinator at the Queens Public Library at Ridgewood. I approach my work in Ridgewood as an exercise in applied anthropology. Queens is the most linguistically diverse place in the world, and working at the library gives me the opportunity to learn more about world languages and cultures.
