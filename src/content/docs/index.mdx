I write educational web content for developers who need to connect Oracle products to other systems. I have been at Oracle since 2021. I own the SOA Suite documentation — the topics in that library — and I maintain the Oracle Integration adapter finder. I transferred to that team from Oracle Health, where I documented known issues, releases, and user workflows. Before that I worked in Oracle Design on research and design teams building a suite of healthcare information management apps. I joined Oracle through its acquisition of Cerner.

I live in Brooklyn. I have a Bachelor of Arts in English Language and Literature from the University of Missouri–Kansas City. As an undergraduate I worked on a research team that investigated rare books at the Spencer Art Reference Library at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art.
