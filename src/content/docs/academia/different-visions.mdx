**Publication:** Different Visions: New Perspectives on Medieval Art, Volume 8  
**Year:** 2022  
**Authors:** Virginia Blanton and Jack Walton (University of Missouri–Kansas City)

An examination of an illuminated manuscript created in 1902 by Joseph Royer depicting the life of Saint Didier, Bishop of Langres. The paper analyzes how Royer's devotional work employs visual and textual rhetoric to construct meanings about masculine sanctity and civic identity, arguing that Didier's martyrdom functions as a metaphor for Langres itself.
