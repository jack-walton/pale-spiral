title: Academic Work
---

I study information organization, music technology, and cultural digitization. My academic research began at the University of Missouri–Kansas City, where I worked as an undergraduate research assistant digitizing a 1904 illuminated manuscript at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art, translating it from French, and co-authoring peer-reviewed work on medieval manuscript studies.

This section collects my academic and research writing: peer-reviewed publications on music information retrieval and medieval studies, a graduate seminar paper on Bandcamp's user experience, and undergraduate research proposals that shaped how I think about archives and access.
