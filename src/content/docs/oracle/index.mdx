## Earlier at Oracle

I started at Cerner in October 2021 and joined Oracle through its acquisition the
following year. From 2023 to 2025, I worked in Oracle Design on research and
design teams building a suite of healthcare information management apps. I then
moved to the Oracle Health product organization, where I documented known issues,
releases, and user workflows until transferring to integrations.

The Oracle Design work is not mine to show. [VENU](/venu/) is a public
demonstration of the kind of content I wrote there: terminology, tasks, errors,
and release notes.
