I am a technical writer at Oracle. My title there is user assistance developer. I write educational web content for developers who need to connect Oracle products to other systems — SOA Suite and Oracle Integration.

## Current work

I work on application and data integrations. In August 2026 I took ownership of
the SOA Suite documentation: the topics in that library, across on-premises,
Kubernetes, and Marketplace releases. The same month I took over the Oracle
Integration adapter finder. Both are public.

The adapter finder is an application on the docs site: a searchable catalog of
every adapter that connects Oracle Integration to an external system. Since I
took it over I replaced an inner scrollbar — which broke readability and
accessibility — with a paginator, made the layout hold up on small screens, and
made search work regardless of word order. I add adapter cards as products
ship. More search-filter categories are next.

## Earlier at Oracle

I started at Cerner in October 2021 and joined Oracle through its acquisition the
following year. From 2023 to 2025, I worked in Oracle Design on research and
design teams building a suite of healthcare information management apps. I then
moved to the Oracle Health product organization, where I documented known issues,
releases, and user workflows until transferring to integrations.

The Oracle Design work is not mine to show. [VENU](/venu/) is a public
demonstration of the kind of content I wrote there: terminology, tasks, and
release notes.
