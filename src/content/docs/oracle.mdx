These products sit at the intersection of healthcare administration and AI — a domain where the stakes of a confusing sentence are higher than usual. Documenting them meant learning the full prior authorization and claims workflow from engineers and clinical consultants, then translating that into guides written for people who are, in most cases, trying to resolve a problem under time pressure. The main judgment call throughout was deciding how much process context to include: enough that a new user understands what they're doing, not so much that an experienced user has to wade through it.

## Denial Management

Denial Management helps Case Managers track and resolve claim denials. An AI agent automatically prepares denial appeal letters for the Case Manager to review. I wrote the Agent Case User Guide and release notes for this product.
