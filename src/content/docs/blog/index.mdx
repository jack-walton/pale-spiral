I designed, built, and still edit [blog.jackwalton.net](https://blog.jackwalton.net/).
It is a personal site I own, and the posts stay there. This page is about the site
as something I built and continue to maintain.

## Why it exists

Two reasons. I wanted a blog platform I own. I wanted a technical project I would
keep going after the novelty of earlier sites wore off.

It updates when I have something worth publishing, which is a few times a year
rather than a few times a month. That is the intended pace.

## Editorial rules

- First person. The writing is my thoughts and experiences, filtered to a level
  of anonymity I am comfortable publishing. The subject matter tends to be
  literary and liminal.
- Kind but not saccharine. I can write about dark things. I cannot write out of
  spite or anger.
- I can delete any post. That keeps me from waiting for a perfect piece.
- I cannot delete the whole site.
