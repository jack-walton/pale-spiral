I designed, built, and still edit [blog.jackwalton.net](https://blog.jackwalton.net/). It is a personal site I own. It is not a feed of posts for this portfolio.

## Why it exists

Two reasons. I wanted a blog platform I own. I wanted a technical project I would keep going, including after I got bored of earlier sites.

## Editorial rules

- First person. The writing is my thoughts and experiences, filtered to a level of anonymity I am comfortable publishing. The subject matter tends to be literary and liminal.
- Kind but not saccharine. I can write about dark things. I cannot write out of spite or anger.
- I can delete any post. That keeps me from waiting for a perfect piece.
- I cannot delete the whole site.

## What this page is not

This is not a mirror of the posts. The posts stay on the blog. This page is about the site as something I built and still maintain.
