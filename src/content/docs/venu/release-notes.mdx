What's new in each version of VENU.

## Version 1.2 (September 3, 2026)

### What's New

- **Your VENU Mobile ID now appears on the Profile screen.** Show the QR code at
  the venue entrance for identity verification.
- **Browse events by date and genre.** The **Discover Events** section filters by
  **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This Weekend**, or **Recently Announced**. The
  **Explore Event Genres** section browses by category.

### Changed

- **The Resale seller fee is now 15%.** It was 20%. Listings created before
  September 3 keep the fee they were created with.
- **Resale earnings now accrue to a balance you can withdraw at any time.**
  Previously, you requested a payout for each listing separately and the request
  had to clear before you could sell again. Your earnings from a sale are
  available 5–7 business days after the event, and the **Transfer to Bank
  Account** button on the **Balance** tab moves the full balance at once.

### Fixed

- Print-at-Home orders no longer appear on **Your Events** with a **View Ticket**
  button that opens an empty barcode. These orders now link to the emailed PDF.
- The six-digit verification code no longer expires while the app is in the
  background.

## Version 1.1 (July 22, 2026)

### What's New

- **Choose how you receive your tickets.** At checkout, select **Mobile** to get
  tickets in the app, or **Print-at-Home** to get a PDF by email.
- **Add Ticket Protection to an order.** On the final purchase screen, select
  **Add** next to **Ticket Protection** for a full refund if you cannot attend
  for a covered reason.

### Changed

- **The Sent tab on Your Events is now called Transferred.** Tickets you sell on
  Resale were landing in the same tab as tickets you handed to a friend. Sold
  tickets now stay on the **Resale** screen, and **Transferred** shows only
  tickets you sent to another person.

### Fixed

- The **Refresh** button on **Your Events** no longer resets you to the
  **Upcoming** tab.
- Event times now display in the venue's local time zone rather than your
  device's. Tickets you already hold may show a corrected start time.

## Version 1.0 (June 12, 2026)

Welcome to VENU. Find events, get tickets, and pass them on when plans change.

### What's New

- Search for events by keyword, genre, or location.
- Buy tickets in three steps: choose your tickets, enter payment, and confirm.
- Sell tickets you cannot use by listing them on Resale.
- Create an account with your email address, or connect an Apple or Facebook account.
- See every ticket you hold on the Your Events screen.
