What's new in each version of VENU.

## Version 1.0 (June 12, 2026)

Welcome to VENU. Find events, get tickets, and pass them on when plans change.

### What's New

- Search for events by keyword, genre, or location.
- Buy tickets in three steps: choose your tickets, enter payment, and confirm.
- Sell tickets you cannot use by listing them on Resale.
- Create an account with your email address, or connect an Apple or Facebook account.
- See every ticket you hold on the Your Events screen.
