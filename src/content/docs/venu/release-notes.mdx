What's new in each version of VENU.

## Version 1.0 (October 3, 2026)

Welcome to VENU. Find events, claim tickets, and pass them on when plans change.

### What's New

- Browse and search events by keyword, genre, and date, built with Oracle JET.
- Claim guest-list tickets on an event page, with no purchase flow and no payment details.
- Transfer tickets to someone else by name and email. They stay in your account until the recipient accepts.
- Track every ticket on the Your Events screen, organized into Upcoming, Transferred, and Past.
- Track incoming and sent transfers on the Transfers screen, with a plain-language explanation of how transfers work.
- A bottom tab bar on small screens keeps navigation reachable without a footer competing for space.
