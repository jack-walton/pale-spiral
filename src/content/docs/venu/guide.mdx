VENU is an app prototype I designed in Figma. The app lets you search for events, purchase tickets, and sell them later if you cannot attend. The guide is written in a shorter, task-focused register for a general mobile audience.

## Navigate the App

The VENU app consists of the following screens:

- Search
- Your Events
- Resale
- Profile

Select the buttons at the bottom of the app to navigate between the **Search**, **Your Events**, and **Resale** screens. Select the profile icon at the top right of the app to access the **Profile** screen.

### Search Screen

The **Search** screen is the main screen of the app. At the top of the screen, a search bar lets you find events, artists, and venues by name. Below the search bar, your current location is displayed. Select the location to change it.

<Image src={searchImg} alt="The VENU Search screen showing Featured Events, Discover Events, and Explore Event Genres sections" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

The **Featured Events** section displays a scrollable carousel of highlighted events in your area. The **Discover Events** section lets you filter events by when they happen — select **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This Weekend**, or **Recently Announced** to narrow the list. The **Explore Event Genres** section lets you browse by category, including Music, Sports, Comedy, Theater, Arts, and Family. Below these sections, the **All Events** list displays all upcoming events in your area.

### Your Events

The **Your Events** screen shows your tickets, organized into three tabs:

- **Upcoming** — events you have tickets for that have not yet happened
- **Transferred** — tickets you have sent to someone else
- **Past** — events that have already occurred

<Image src={yourEventsImg} alt="The Your Events screen showing an upcoming Taylor Swift ticket with section, row, and seat information" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

Each ticket card displays the event name, date, time, venue, and your seat details — including section, row, and seat numbers. Select **View Ticket** to open the ticket barcode, which you present at the venue entrance. If your tickets are not displaying, select **Refresh**.

### Resale

The **Resale** screen lets you sell tickets you can no longer use. The screen is organized into three tabs:

- **Active Listings** — tickets you currently have listed for sale
- **Sold** — tickets you have already sold
- **Balance** — your earnings from ticket sales

<Image src={resaleImg} alt="The Resale screen showing a $0.00 sales balance, a Transfer to Bank Account button, and a How Resale Works section" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

The **Balance** tab displays your available earnings and a **Transfer to Bank Account** button. The **How Resale Works** section explains the resale process: list your tickets, receive payment within 5–7 business days after the event, and withdraw your balance at any time.

<Aside type="note">
VENU charges a 15% seller fee on all resale transactions. Prices are set by sellers and may exceed face value.
</Aside>

### Profile

Select the profile icon at the top right of the app to open the **Profile** screen.

<Image src={profileImg} alt="The Profile screen showing the VENU Mobile ID QR code, Quick Access links, Settings, and Help & More sections" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

At the top of the screen, your **VENU Mobile ID** is displayed as a QR code. Show this code at the venue entrance for identity verification — do not share it with others.

The **Quick Access** section links to your **Notifications** and **Order History**. The **Settings** section lets you manage your location services, home location, account details, app appearance, cookie preferences, and notification settings. The **Help & More** section links to VENU Support, FAQs, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use. Select **Log Out** at the bottom to sign out of the app.

## Create an Account

Complete the following steps to create a VENU account.

<Steps>
1. **Enter Your Information**

   From the landing screen, select **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Facebook** to register using an existing account. Otherwise, select **Sign Up with Email** to register with your email address.

   Enter your first name, last name, email address, and a password. The password must be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one uppercase letter and one number.

   Select the checkbox to confirm that you may be required to show valid ID at venues and that you agree to VENU's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Then select **Create Account**.

   <Image src={createStep1Img} alt="The Your Info sign-up form with fields for first name, last name, email address, and password" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

2. **Verify Your Email**

   VENU sends a verification link to the email address you entered. Open your email inbox and select the link. After you have verified your email, return to the app and select **I Verified My Email — Continue**.

   <Image src={createStep2Img} alt="The Verify Email screen instructing the user to select the link sent to their email address" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

3. **Verify Your Phone Number**

   Enter your phone number, then select **Send Code Via Text**.

   <Image src={createStep3Img} alt="The Phone Verification screen with a phone number field and a Send Code Via Text button" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

   Enter the six-digit code sent to your phone number, then select **Complete Sign Up**.

   <Image src={createStep4Img} alt="The Phone Verification screen after sending a code, with a field for the six-digit code and a Complete Sign Up button" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />
</Steps>

## Sign In

Complete the following steps to sign in to the app.

<Steps>
1. **Enter Your Credentials**

   From the landing screen, select **Already have an account? Log In**. Enter your email address and password, then select **Continue**.

   <Image src={signInStep1Img} alt="The Log In screen with fields for email address and password and a Continue button" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

   If you have forgotten your password, select **Forgot password? Reset it**.

2. **Verify Your Account**

   Enter the six-digit verification code sent to the phone number on your account to complete sign-in.
</Steps>

## Search for Events

Complete the following steps to search for events.

<Steps>
1. **Start Your Search**

   From the **Search** screen, select the search bar at the top of the screen. Enter an event name, artist, or venue. Results appear as you type.

   <Image src={searchResultsImg} alt="The Search screen showing search results for Taylor Swift" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

   You can also browse events without searching. Use the **Discover Events** filters — **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This Weekend**, or **Recently Announced** — to narrow events by date. Select a genre from **Explore Event Genres** to browse by category. See the [Search Screen](#search-screen) section for more information.

2. **Select an Event**

   Select an event from the results or browse list to open its detail page. The event detail page displays the event name, date, time, venue, and available ticket options.

   <Image src={eventDetailImg} alt="The event detail page for Taylor Swift | The Eras Tour showing date, time, venue, and ticket options" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

   Two ticket types are available:

   - **Tickets** — standard admission at face value price
   - **VENU Official Resale** — verified fan-to-fan resale tickets, which may exceed face value

   To purchase, select **Buy Now** and follow the steps in [Buy Tickets](#buy-tickets).
</Steps>

## Buy Tickets

Complete the following steps to buy tickets.

<Steps>
1. **Select Offer**

   After you search for an event and select **Buy Now**, the **Select Offer** screen opens.

   Under **Ticket Type**, select **Tickets** to purchase at face value, or select **VENU Official Resale** to buy verified fan-to-fan resale tickets. Use the **Quantity** selector to choose how many tickets you want. Under **Delivery Method**, select **Mobile** to receive tickets in the VENU app, or select **Print-at-Home** to receive a PDF by email.

   <Image src={buyStep1Img} alt="The Select Offer screen showing ticket type options, a quantity selector, and delivery method options" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

   Select **Continue**.

2. **Enter Payment Information**

   Enter your credit or debit card number, expiry date, CVV, and the name on the card. Optionally, select the checkbox to save this payment method for future purchases.

   <Image src={buyStep2Img} alt="The Payment screen with fields for card number, expiry date, CVV, and name on card" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />

   Select **Continue**.

3. **Complete Purchase**

   Enter your billing address. Optionally, select **Add** next to **Ticket Protection** to purchase coverage that provides a full refund if you cannot attend due to illness, injury, or other covered reasons.

   Review the **Order Summary**, which lists your ticket quantities, prices, service fee, and total. If you agree to VENU's Terms of Use and confirm the order details are correct, select the checkbox and then select **Confirm Purchase**.

   <Image src={buyStep3Img} alt="The Complete Purchase screen showing billing address fields, ticket protection option, and an order summary" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} quality={95} />
</Steps>
