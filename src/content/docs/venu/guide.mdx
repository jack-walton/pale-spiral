VENU is a web app for finding events and claiming guest-list tickets. If you cannot attend, you transfer your tickets to someone else instead of letting them go to waste. The guide is written in a shorter, task-focused register for a general audience.

<Image src={signInImg} alt="The VENU sign-in screen with an email field, an Email me a link button, and a Continue as guest button" />

The first time you open VENU, you land on **Sign In**. Enter your email to receive a magic link, or select **Continue as guest** to explore with a small set of sample tickets already loaded — no account required. Return visits skip straight to Discover and pick up where your session left off.

<Image src={discoverImg} alt="The VENU Discover screen showing search, date and genre filters, and a grid of featured events" />

## Navigate the App

VENU consists of three sections, listed in the sidebar:

- **Discover** — find events
- **Your events** — tickets you hold
- **Transfers** — tickets sent to you and tickets you have sent

Select your account menu in the top right to view your **Profile**, read the **User Guide** or **Release Notes**, **Start Over** to reset the sample data, or **Sign Out**.

### Discover

The **Discover** screen is the main screen of the app. A search bar at the top lets you find events by name, artist, or venue. Below it, **When** filters narrow the list to **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This Weekend**, or **Recently Announced**, and **Genre** filters narrow it to **Music**, **Sports**, **Comedy**, **Theater**, **Arts**, or **Family**.

The **Featured in your area** section highlights a short list of events. Below it, the full event list shows every upcoming event that matches your filters, with its genre, neighborhood, date, time, and venue.

### Your Events

The **Your Events** screen shows the tickets in your session, organized into three tabs:

- **Upcoming** — tickets for events that have not yet happened
- **Transferred** — tickets you have sent to someone else
- **Past** — tickets for events that have already occurred

Each ticket lists the event name, date, time, venue, and seat details when they apply. Select **View Ticket** to open the ticket's door code, or **Transfer** to send it to someone else.

<Image src={yourEventsImg} alt="The Your Events screen showing two upcoming tickets: Brooklyn Nets vs. New York Knicks with seat details, and Japanese Breakfast" />

### Transfers

The **Transfers** screen tracks tickets moving between accounts, organized into three tabs:

- **Incoming** — tickets someone has sent you, waiting on your decision
- **Sent** — tickets you have sent that are still waiting on the recipient
- **Completed** — transfers that have finished

<Image src={transfersImg} alt="The Transfers screen showing one incoming transfer for The National, from Jordan Hale, with Accept and Decline buttons" />

An incoming transfer shows the sender's name, the event, and any message they included. Select **Accept tickets** to add it to your Upcoming tab, or **Decline** to turn it down. A short explanation of how transfers work is always visible below the list.

### Profile

Select your account menu, then **Profile**, to open the Profile screen.

<Image src={profileImg} alt="The Profile screen showing a VENU Mobile ID QR code, Quick Access links, Account session details, and a Help and more section" />

At the top, your **VENU Mobile ID** displays as a QR code — show this at the venue entrance for identity verification, alongside a photo ID. The **Account** section shows your session type, email, phone, and home location. The **Help and more** section links to the **User Guide** and **Release Notes**, and lets you **Start Over** to restore the sample tickets you began with.

## Sign In

Complete the following steps to sign in to VENU.

<Steps>
1. **Request a Link**

   On the Sign In screen, enter your email address and select **Email me a link**. VENU has no password — if you would rather skip this, select **Continue as guest** instead to explore with sample tickets.

2. **Open the Link**

   VENU sends a sign-in link to your email. The link expires in 15 minutes. Open it in the same browser to finish signing in.

   <Aside type="note">
   If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or select **Send a new link**.
   </Aside>
</Steps>

## Claim Tickets

Complete the following steps to claim tickets for an event.

<Steps>
1. **Find an Event**

   From **Discover**, search or browse to find an event. Select it to open the event page.

2. **Claim Your Tickets**

   On the event page, the **Claim tickets** panel explains that VENU holds a guest list for the event. Use the **Quantity** selector to choose how many tickets you want — up to the limit shown for that event — then select **Claim [N] ticket(s)**.

   <Image src={claimImg} alt="An event page for boygenius with a Claim tickets panel, a quantity selector set to 1, and a Claim 1 ticket button" />

   <Aside type="note">
   Tickets are free to claim. VENU asks you to claim only what you will use, since unclaimed tickets stay available for other guests.
   </Aside>

3. **Present Your Ticket**

   Open the ticket from **Your Events** to see its door code.

   <Image src={ticketScanImg} alt="A claimed ticket for boygenius showing a QR code to scan at the door, the seat section, and delivery method" />

   Show this code and your VENU Mobile ID at the venue entrance. Do not screenshot a code during a transfer in progress — the receiving account holds the live ticket only after they accept.
</Steps>

## Transfer Tickets

If you cannot attend an event, transfer your ticket instead of leaving it unused.

<Steps>
1. **Start the Transfer**

   From **Your Events** or a ticket's detail page, select **Transfer**.

2. **Choose Tickets and Recipient**

   Select one or more tickets to include. Enter the recipient's name and email address, and optionally add a message.

3. **Review and Send**

   Confirm you understand that the tickets stay in your account until the recipient accepts, then select **Send transfer**.

   The transfer appears under the **Sent** tab until the recipient accepts or declines. You can cancel it any time before then. Once accepted, it moves to **Completed** and the ticket leaves your account.
</Steps>

## Accept an Incoming Transfer

Complete the following steps to accept tickets someone has sent you.

<Steps>
1. **Open Transfers**

   Select **Transfers** in the sidebar. Tickets waiting for your decision appear under the **Incoming** tab, with the sender's name and any message they included.

2. **Accept or Decline**

   Select **Accept tickets** to add the tickets to your **Upcoming** tab, or **Decline** to turn down the transfer.
</Steps>
