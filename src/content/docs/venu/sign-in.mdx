Complete the following steps to sign in to the app.

<Steps>
1. **Access the Sign In Screen**

   From the landing screen, select **Already have an account? Log In**.

2. **Enter Your Information**

   Enter your email address and password, then select **Continue**.

   <Image src={step1Img} alt="The Log In screen with fields for email address and password and a Continue button" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} />

   If you have forgotten your password, select **Forgot password? Reset it**.

3. **Verify Your Account**

   Enter the six-digit verification code sent to your phone number to complete sign-in.
</Steps>
