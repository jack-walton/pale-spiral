The VENU app consists of the following screens:

- Search
- Your Events
- Resale
- Profile

Select the buttons at the bottom of the app to navigate between the **Search**, **Your Events**, and **Resale** screens. Select the profile icon at the top right of the app to access the **Profile** screen.

## Search

The **Search** screen is the main screen of the app. At the top of the screen, a search bar lets you find events, artists, and venues by name. Below the search bar, your current location is displayed. Select the location to change it.

<Image src={searchImg} alt="The VENU Search screen showing Featured Events, Discover Events, and Explore Event Genres sections" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} />

The **Featured Events** section displays a scrollable carousel of highlighted events in your area. The **Discover Events** section lets you filter events by when they happen — select **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This Weekend**, or **Recently Announced** to narrow the list. The **Explore Event Genres** section lets you browse by category, including Music, Sports, Comedy, Theater, Arts, and Family. Below these sections, the **All Events** list displays all upcoming events in your area.

## Your Events

The **Your Events** screen shows your tickets, organized into three tabs:

- **Upcoming** — events you have tickets for that have not yet happened
- **Transferred** — tickets you have sent to someone else
- **Past** — events that have already occurred

<Image src={yourEventsImg} alt="The Your Events screen showing an upcoming Taylor Swift ticket with section, row, and seat information" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} />

Each ticket card displays the event name, date, time, venue, and your seat details — including section, row, and seat numbers. Select **View Ticket** to open the ticket barcode, which you present at the venue entrance. If your tickets are not displaying, select **Refresh**.

## Resale

The **Resale** screen lets you sell tickets you can no longer use. The screen is organized into three tabs:

- **Active Listings** — tickets you currently have listed for sale
- **Sold** — tickets you have already sold
- **Balance** — your earnings from ticket sales

<Image src={resaleImg} alt="The Resale screen showing a $0.00 sales balance, a Transfer to Bank Account button, and a How Resale Works section" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} />

The **Balance** tab displays your available earnings and a **Transfer to Bank Account** button. The **How Resale Works** section explains the resale process: list your tickets, receive payment within 5–7 business days after the event, and withdraw your balance at any time.

<Aside type="note">
VENU charges a 15% seller fee on all resale transactions. Prices are set by sellers and may exceed face value.
</Aside>

## Profile

Select the profile icon at the top right of the app to open the **Profile** screen.

<Image src={profileImg} alt="The Profile screen showing the VENU Mobile ID QR code, Quick Access links, Settings, and Help & More sections" width={390} height={390} densities={[1, 2]} />

At the top of the screen, your **VENU Mobile ID** is displayed as a QR code. Show this code at the venue entrance for identity verification — do not share it with others.

The **Quick Access** section links to your **Notifications** and **Order History**. The **Settings** section lets you manage your location services, home location, account details, app appearance, cookie preferences, and notification settings. The **Help & More** section links to VENU Support, FAQs, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use. Select **Log Out** at the bottom to sign out of the app.
