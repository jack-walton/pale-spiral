## Why this project exists

VENU is not a shipped app. It is a speculative ticketing product I designed in Figma, with a user guide and release notes written for that prototype. I published it because I cannot show the healthcare apps I worked on in Oracle Design. The content problems are the same: terminology, task flows, empty and error states, and what to say when the product changes.
