Complete the following steps to sign in to the app.

<Steps>
1. **Access the Sign In Screen**

   Access the **Profile** screen. See [Navigate the App](/axs/navigate-the-app/) for more information about how to access the **Profile** screen. Select **Log In or Sign Up**, then tap **Log In**.

   ![The Log In or Sign Up screen with the Log In option selected](/images/AXS/sign_in/sign_in_step_1.jpg)

2. **Enter Your Information**

   Enter your email address and password. Then, select **Continue**.

   ![The email address and password entry form](/images/AXS/sign_in/sign_in_step_2.jpg)

3. **Verify Your Account**

   Enter your phone number then select **Send Code Via Text** to verify your account. Then, enter the six-digit code that was sent to your phone number.

   ![The phone number entry screen with a Send Code Via Text button](/images/AXS/sign_in/sign_in_step_3.jpg)
</Steps>
