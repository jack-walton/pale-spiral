I built and maintain this site as a professional portfolio — a digital business
card with room to actually read something. I had three audiences in mind:

- People from LinkedIn
- People who search my name and location
- People I meet randomly in real life

The splash page is who I am. These pages are the work. The format is Starlight
because documentation is the kind of system I write for, and a portfolio that
demonstrates the craft beats one that describes it.

## How I built it

The site is Astro and Starlight, hosted on Vercel. The header wordmark uses Zen
Dots. Body copy and headings use Zen Kaku Gothic New, and code and UI accents use
Geist Mono. Custom CSS is limited to type, splash layout, and a few Starlight
overrides. I kept the rest of the chrome: search, theme toggle, and socials.

## What lives where

Each section here follows one rule: work stays wherever it was published, and
this site describes it and links out.

- Oracle writing stays on [docs.oracle.com](https://docs.oracle.com/). This site
  describes the work and links to it.
- VENU is a speculative product. It stands in for UX content I wrote in Oracle
  Design that I cannot show. The guide and release notes describe a Figma
  prototype.
- Academic papers that already have a publisher stay at the publisher. I
  summarize them here and link. Unpublished academic work is reproduced in full.
- The blog is a separate site I own. Posts stay there. The page on this site is
  about the blog as something I built.
