## Why it looks like this

I had three audiences in mind: people from LinkedIn, people who search my name, and people I meet in real life. Those audiences are in tension. A recruiter is a different visitor than an acquaintance looking for Instagram. The splash leads with the public facts — work, school, libraries. Socials are in the header. The work is in these pages.

The site is Astro and Starlight, hosted on Vercel. The wordmark is Zen Dots. Body copy and headings are Zen Kaku Gothic New. Code uses Geist Mono. The stack I use for this kind of work is Markdown, Git, Vale, Figma, Astro, and Starlight.
