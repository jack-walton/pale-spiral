I built this site as a portfolio, and as user assistance for my own work. The format is Starlight because that is the kind of system I write for: a splash for identity, a sidebar for artifacts, and pages that can hold a case study, a task guide, or a paper.

## Purpose

The splash is who I am. These pages are the work. I did not want a gallery of screenshots with no reading, and I did not want a blog pretending to be a resume.

## How I built it

The site is Astro and Starlight, hosted on Vercel. The header wordmark uses Zen Dots. Body copy and headings use Zen Kaku Gothic New. Custom CSS is limited to type, splash layout, and a few Starlight overrides. I kept the rest of the chrome: search, theme toggle, and socials.

## Considerations

- Oracle writing stays on [docs.oracle.com](https://docs.oracle.com/). This site links out and describes the work. It does not republish it.
- VENU is a speculative product. It stands in for UX content I wrote in Oracle Design that I cannot show. The guide and release notes describe a Figma prototype.
- Academic papers that already have a publisher stay at the publisher. I summarize them here and link.
- The blog is a separate site I own. Posts stay there. The page on this site is about the blog as something I built.
